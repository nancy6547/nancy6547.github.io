Zeitaku playground

Paste a Playwright test. Zeitaku parses it with the TypeScript compiler, finds every hard-coded waitForTimeout, checks whether each sleep is guarding a "should NOT happen" assertion, and rewrites only the safe ones into a network-aware wait. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
